## Sending a Quote to a Client

Once your quote is ready:

1. Open the quote
2. Click **Send to Client** button
3. Review the pre-filled email
4. Click **Send**

The quote status changes from **Draft** to **Sent** automatically.

## What the Client Receives

The client receives an email with a link to view the quote in their client portal.

When the client clicks the link they can:
1. Review all line items and pricing
2. Download the PDF
3. Accept and digitally sign the quote

## Quote Accepted

When the client accepts and signs:
- Quote status changes to **Accepted** automatically
- A job is created instantly with all line items
- Your staff see the update in real time
