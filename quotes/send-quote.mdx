Once a quote is built, send it to the customer via the built-in email or by sharing a portal link directly.

## Sending via email

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click 'Send to Client'">
    From the quote detail screen, click **Send to Client** at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the email">
    A pre-filled email composer opens. The subject and body are in the customer's preferred language.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit if needed">
    You can change the subject, body, or add a CC. The portal link is auto-included.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send**. The quote moves from **Draft** to **Sent** and the customer receives the email.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The default email template:

> **Subject**: Quote {QT-YYYY-NNNN} from {Your Company}
>
> Dear {Customer Contact},
>
> Please find your quote attached. You can review and accept it through the secure link below:
>
> {Portal Link}
>
> The quote is valid until {Expiry Date}. Please let us know if you have any questions.
>
> Best regards,
> {Your Name}

You can change the default template in **Settings → Business Settings → Email Templates**.

## What the client receives

The email includes:

- The PDF of the quote (bilingual, EN/TH side by side)
- A portal link button labeled **View & Accept Quote**
- Your company's contact info in the signature

When they click the portal link, they land on the quote view in the client portal. From there they can:

1. Read the quote
2. Download the PDF
3. Accept and digitally sign
4. Pay the deposit (if required)

If they reply to the email, the reply goes to your company email inbox — not into JingjaiOps.

## Portal link in the email

The portal link is unique to this quote. It works without a password — anyone with the link can view, accept, and sign. Treat it like a payment link.

Link format:

```
https://portal.jingjaiops.com/q/{token}
```

The link expires when the quote is accepted, declined, or hits its expiry date.

## Following up

To check whether the customer has opened the quote:

1. Open the quote in JingjaiOps.
2. Look at the **Activity** sidebar.

You'll see entries like:

- `Sent to client@example.com — 14 Mar 2026 10:32`
- `Opened by client — 14 Mar 2026 10:48`
- `Portal viewed — 14 Mar 2026 10:48`
- `Accepted by Somchai Charoen — 14 Mar 2026 11:12`

If the customer hasn't opened the quote in a couple of days, follow up. The **Send Reminder** action sends a friendly chase email with the same portal link.

## Quote expiry

Every quote has an expiry date (defaults to 14 days from creation). After expiry:

- The quote moves to **Expired** status.
- The portal link still loads, but the customer sees a message saying the quote has expired.
- They can no longer accept it.

To revive an expired quote, open it and click **Extend Expiry**. Pick a new date and the customer can accept again. Or duplicate the quote to issue a fresh one with a new number.

<Tip>
  If you set the expiry too short and the customer wants to accept after it expires, just extend it — there is no downside, and the original quote number is preserved.
</Tip>
