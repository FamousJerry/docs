JingjaiOps is the operations platform for rental businesses. It replaces spreadsheets, paper job sheets, and disconnected accounting tools with a single workflow that covers every stage of a rental — from the first quote to the final receipt.

## The core rental workflow

Every rental moves through the same five stages. JingjaiOps tracks each one and keeps your team, your warehouse, and your client in sync.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Quote">
    Build a quote from inventory or kits, send it for digital signature, and collect a security deposit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Job">
    A signed quote becomes a job. The job tracks every stage from pickup to return.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick & Scan">
    Scan equipment out of the warehouse with a barcode scanner. Kits expand into individual items.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deliver & Run">
    Mark the job loaded, in transit, and on site. The client sees real-time status in their portal.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return & Invoice">
    Scan items back in, mark condition, generate the AI job report, and issue the final invoice.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

- **Bilingual** — Every quote, invoice, and portal page renders in English or Thai.
- **Real-time** — Job stage changes update for every staff member and the client portal instantly.
- **Thai compliance** — Tax invoices (ใบกำกับภาษี), VAT handling, and PromptPay QR codes built in.
- **Mobile first** — The client portal works on any phone, no app install required.
- **AI throughout** — Claude AI powers quote suggestions, job reports, and natural language reporting.
