Notifications in JingjaiOps tell the right people the moment something needs attention — most importantly, when an item comes back damaged or doesn't come back at all.

## Damage and missing alerts

When you mark an item **Damaged** or **Missing** during Return & Inspect, JingjaiOps fires an alert email to a configured list of recipients. This is the most important automation in the app — it pulls in operations, accounting, or insurance contacts immediately rather than waiting for someone to log in.

## Adding email recipients

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Notifications">
    From the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Add Recipient'">
    A form appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the email">
    Any valid email address — does not need to be a JingjaiOps user.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the trigger">
    Damaged, Missing, or Both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the language">
    EN or TH for the alert email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    The recipient is now active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add as many recipients as you need. Common patterns:

- Operations Lead — gets all damaged + missing alerts
- Accounting — gets only Missing alerts (so they know to forfeit deposits)
- Insurance Broker — gets damaged alerts above a certain value
- Owner — gets all alerts in summary form

## Trigger options

Each recipient is configured to receive one of:

| Trigger | When the alert fires |
| --- | --- |
| **Damaged** | Item marked Damaged at inspection |
| **Missing** | Item marked Missing at inspection |
| **Both** | Either condition triggers an alert |

You can have one recipient on Damaged-only and another on Missing-only — flexibility per recipient.

## What's in the alert email

The alert includes:

- Subject: "🚨 Damage / Missing alert: {Job Number}"
- Customer name
- Job number and dates
- Item that was flagged (asset ID, item name)
- Condition (Damaged or Missing)
- Notes you typed at inspection
- Photos attached to the inspection (rendered inline)
- Estimated cost (if you typed one)
- A button **Open Job in JingjaiOps**

The email is sent in the recipient's configured language (EN or TH).

## Enable / disable globally

If you want to temporarily silence alerts (e.g., during a known maintenance period where you'll be marking many items as damaged for routine reasons):

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Notifications">
    Top of the page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle 'Damage Alerts'">
    Off — no alerts fire. On — alerts fire to configured recipients.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The toggle is global. When you turn it back on, future damage events fire normally; missed events during the off period are not retroactively sent.

## Per-customer suppression

For some customers (a long-term contract where damage is expected and handled separately), you may want alerts off for that customer only:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer">
    From the Customers list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open 'Notification Preferences'">
    Bottom of the customer profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle 'Suppress damage alerts'">
    Damage events on this customer's jobs will not fire alerts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Even with alerts suppressed, the damage is still recorded on the job and visible in reports. You're just not paging anyone about it.

## Other notification types

JingjaiOps also sends notifications for:

| Event | Recipient |
| --- | --- |
| New recurring invoice draft ready | User who set up the schedule, plus Billing role |
| Quote accepted by customer | Quote owner, Sales role |
| Invoice paid in full | Invoice owner, Billing role |
| Customer reported PromptPay payment | Billing role |
| User invitation sent | Inviter |

These notifications go to in-app notifications (the bell icon in the top bar) and are NOT configurable per user. They go to whoever has the appropriate role.

## In-app vs email

| Channel | When |
| --- | --- |
| In-app | Most events. Visible from the bell icon, persists until read |
| Email | Damage / missing alerts only by default |

If you want email for additional events (e.g., quote accepted), enable it in **Settings → Notifications → Email Preferences**. Defaults are conservative because most teams don't want every event in their inbox.

## Notification log

Every notification sent is logged in **Settings → Notifications → Log**:

| Sent at | Recipient | Type | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 14 Mar 2026 17:48 | ops@you.com | Damage Alert | Delivered |
| 14 Mar 2026 17:48 | accounting@you.com | Damage Alert | Delivered |

Click any row to see the full email content. Useful for debugging "I didn't get the alert" complaints.
